Frequently Asked Questions

How do I become a dealer?+
Click "Dealer Login" on the login page and sign in with any email/password — a dealer account is created automatically the first time.
How does checkout work?+
Add items to your cart, go to the Cart page, fill in your delivery details, and confirm. This creates a real order in the database that the dealer(s) can see and fulfill.
Can I track my order status?+
Yes — visit "My Orders" any time. Dealers update the status (Order Placed → Packed → Shipped → Out for Delivery → Delivered) from their dashboard, and you'll see it reflected there.
How do I contact a seller?+
Open any product's details page and click "Message Seller" to start a live chat.
Is my data saved permanently?+
Yes — accounts, products, orders, wishlists, and messages are all stored in a shared SQL database on the backend, not just in your browser.
